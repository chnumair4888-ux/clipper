Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

AI Gaming Clipper helps users create short gaming video clips. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the service.

Information We Process

Information may include video URLs submitted by users, clip settings, captions, language preferences, and generated video files. If Instagram publishing is enabled and authorized by a user, Instagram account identifiers and access tokens may also be processed.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to process videos, generate clips, provide requested features, troubleshoot errors, and publish content only when the user has authorized that action.

Third-Party Services

The service may use GitHub Actions, n8n, Google Gemini, video hosting providers, and Meta/Instagram APIs. These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Storage and Security

Video files, workflow data, logs, and credentials may be stored or processed by the services used to operate the application. Reasonable steps are taken to protect credentials and information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Sharing

Personal information is not sold. Information may be processed by the service providers listed above or disclosed when legally required.

Your Choices

You can stop using the service and request deletion of information associated with your use by contacting us. You can also revoke Instagram permissions through Meta or Instagram settings.

Children's Privacy

The service is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
