# Music library and license records

Place only tracks you have verified are permitted for your intended social-media use in the matching folder:

- `music/viral/`
- `music/funny/`
- `music/cinematic/`
- `music/esports/`

For every track, record at minimum:
- Exact filename
- Track title and creator
- Original source URL
- License name and a link to its full terms
- Whether commercial use, edits, and social-platform monetization are permitted
- Any required attribution text

Do not add a track unless its license and source have been checked. A label such as "free" or "no copyright" is not enough. Keep proof of the license with the project.

The clipper supports `music` and `original+music` modes. If no matching licensed track exists, it logs a note and preserves the existing audio rather than downloading or selecting music automatically.
